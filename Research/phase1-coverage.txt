=== Key Finding ===
North Sami (se) and all other Sami languages receive ONLY the three lowest
tier schemes: Language, Script, TokenType.
This means Dictionary.app and Spotlight cannot perform morphology-aware
lookup for these languages via the standard NLTagger pipeline.

Contrast: Japanese (ja), Chinese (zh), Korean (ko) have full tokenization
support despite being morphologically complex languages.
English, French, German, Portuguese, Russian and Swedish have .lemma support.
All other languages—including Norwegian Nynorsk and most minority languages—do not.
